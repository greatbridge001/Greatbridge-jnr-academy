About us

A legacy of learning

Founded in 1998, Green Valley Academy has grown from a small community school into an institution known for academic rigour, compassionate teaching and a steady commitment to every learner’s growth.

We believe education reaches beyond the classroom. Our approach builds curiosity, social responsibility and personal confidence so that learners can thrive.

  • Academic excellenceA challenging curriculum designed to stretch potential.
  • Global perspectivePreparing learners for an interconnected world.
  • Innovation mindsetCreativity and critical thinking in every subject.
At Green Valley, we believe every child carries a spark of greatness. Our mission is simply to fan that flame.

Dr. James Kariuki
School Principal

Academics

A strong foundation for a brighter future

Green Valley delivers Kenya’s Competency Based Curriculum: learner-centred lessons, values and character, practical skills, and continuous assessment through portfolios and projects.

School rules

Attendance & punctuality
  • Gates open at 6:30 AM. Learners must be in class by 7:30 AM.
  • Absence needs a written explanation from a parent or guardian.
  • More than 3 late arrivals in a term leads to a parent meeting.
Uniform & appearance
  • Full uniform is worn every day; sports kit only on PE days.
  • Hair must be neat and natural. No jewellery, makeup or nail polish.
Electronics & devices
  • Day scholars may not use phones during school hours.
  • Boarders hand in phones by 9:00 PM each night.
Academic conduct
  • Assignments are submitted on the deadline.
  • Plagiarism or cheating means a zero mark and disciplinary action.
Behaviour & respect
  • Every learner treats others with courtesy and respect.
  • Bullying, verbal abuse and physical aggression are not tolerated.
Boarding rules
  • In dorms by 9:30 PM; lights out at 10:00 PM.
  • Visiting is Saturdays, 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM, with gate sign-in.
  • Leaving campus needs written permission from school and parent.
